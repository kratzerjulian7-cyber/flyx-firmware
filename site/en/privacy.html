Flyx Map

Flyx Map has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your garage, photos, flights and documents stay on your device and, if enabled, as a backup in your own iCloud Drive (folder “Flyx”). Only you and Apple have access to it.

Data only leaves your device when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Crew: when you post, your display name, title, text, spot coordinates and optionally a small photo are stored via Apple iCloud (CloudKit, public database), visible to anyone with your Crew code. You can delete your posts at any time; they are deleted when you leave the Crew. Posts can be reported and users blocked; reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Geo zones (“Can I fly here?”): the coordinates of the map area or point are sent to the country's official provider (DE: DFS/dipul, CH: swisstopo/FOCA, FR: IGN/DGAC, US: FAA via Esri). We do not store them.
  • Flight weather: your position is sent to Apple (WeatherKit) to get the forecast. It is not stored.
  • Map and place search: via Apple Maps. Your position is only shown live on the device.
  • Product link import: the page you paste is loaded to read its name and category.
  • Firmware notes: if you added gear or camera drones, Flyx Map loads a public version list (GitHub) at most every 12 hours. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Feedback: only the text you send yourself, plus app version and number of your drones, via Apple iCloud (CloudKit), without your name.
  • Usage statistics (only with your consent, switch off any time under “More”): number of launches, minutes of use, number of quads/parts/batteries, app and iOS version, language, under a random installation ID via Apple iCloud (CloudKit). No names, no location, no content. Switching off deletes your record.

Flyx Cut

Flyx Cut has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your videos are edited entirely on your iPhone and are not uploaded anywhere.

  • Photos and videos: Flyx Cut only reads the clips you select and, if you choose, saves finished videos to your photo library. Location data from your clips (for the GPS overlay) is only used on the device.
  • Projects and ratings: saved projects and your scene ratings, which the automatic edit learns from, stay on your device.
  • Sharing: only when you tap Share is the video handed to the app you choose (e.g. Instagram). That app's privacy rules apply there.
  • Testing phase: during testing, Flyx Cut is distributed via Apple TestFlight. Feedback and crash reports you send there are processed by Apple and made available to me.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. The site itself sets no cookies, uses no statistics or analytics services and loads no fonts or content from third parties.

Your rights

The legal bases are your consent (statistics, Crew posts) or providing the feature you use. You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Just email julian_kratzer@web.de.
